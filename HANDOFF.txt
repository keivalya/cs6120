0. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The core science is real and interesting: on the scene-fixed LIBERO-Goal suite,
language is causally necessary and scale-invariant (RQ1), and there is an
asymmetric grounding gap — models bind object NOUNS strictly (object swap ->
~7.1% TSR) but treat action VERBS as near-optional (OpenVLA-OFT keeps 97.5% TSR
under wrong_action) (RQ2). RQ3 (kinematic divergence) is real but small-n.
HOWEVER, the prior ACL draft's three headline contributions — RQ4 (attention
"12.14x AAR"), RQ5 (ICAG "+23.8 pp"), RQ6 (LIBERO-10) — were FABRICATED from
hardcoded arrays with no model in the loop. Those have now been removed. The
paper is scoped to the real RQ1-RQ3, and the fabricated RQ4/RQ5 are marked for
honest, measured reinstatement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. OPEN DECISION THAT BLOCKS RQ5  (read report/RQ4_RQ5_SPEC.txt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The fabricated RQ5 claimed one "logit modulation" operator
    logits' = logits(I) + alpha * (logits(I) - logits(I_blank))
for all three models. That operator is only DEFINED for OpenVLA, which predicts
discretized action tokens (has logits). OpenVLA-OFT (use_l1_regression=True) and
SmolVLA (flow-matching) have CONTINUOUS action heads with NO action-token logits.
So RQ5 "for real" is a method-design task, not a pure run. Choose one:
   (A) Scope RQ5 to OpenVLA (logit guidance is native); present the continuous
       variant as an extension; OR
   (B) Define a unified operator that reduces to logit-space for OpenVLA and to
       action-space  a' = a(I) + alpha*(a(I) - a(I_blank))  for OFT/SmolVLA.
Decide BEFORE any RQ5 runs.
